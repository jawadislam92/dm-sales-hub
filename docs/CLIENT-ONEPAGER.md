# Your Zapier bill — what's really going on, and how we shrink it without losing anything

**The short version.** You're paying for a plan that's actually big enough. The problem isn't the plan — it's the *spikes*. Some months your bill jumps to around **$3,260** because a flood of tiny automated steps runs behind the scenes. Most of those steps just copy numbers into a spreadsheet or send an internal "FYI." That's exactly the work your new dashboard does for you automatically. Turn those steps off — only *after* the dashboard has proven it does the same job — and the spikes disappear.

Nothing here is a rip-and-replace. We build the dashboard *alongside* everything you run today. You decide how far to go, and every step is reversible.

---

## Where the money actually goes

Your Zapier plan includes **100,000 automated "tasks" a month for a flat $898.50**. Go over that, and every extra task costs about **2 cents**.

That one fact explains your whole bill:

- In a **quiet month**, you stayed *under* 100,000 tasks and paid *less* than the base — one recent cycle came in around $658.
- In a **busy month**, you ran roughly **100,000 tasks *over*** the allowance. That's the **$3,260** bill. Nothing broke and nothing got bigger — there were just more tiny steps firing.

So the fix was never "buy a bigger plan." The fix is **fewer wasted tasks**. And the tasks that pile up in the expensive months are the mechanical ones — copying a number into a spreadsheet, posting an internal alert, mirroring a record from one tool into another. None of that reaches a customer. All of it is work the dashboard and your own database can do for free.

## You're in control: keep the sheet, or move off it — your call

This is the part that matters most. **Nothing is forced, and nothing happens until you trust it.**

- Your Google Sheet keeps working **exactly as it does today.** We don't touch it.
- The dashboard's numbers already **match your HUB totals to the dollar** — we checked June against your own team's HUB and it reconciles precisely, right down to individual reps' close rates.
- Only when *you're* satisfied the new numbers are right do we move to the next step. And if anything ever looks off, the sheet is still sitting right there as your safety net.

You set the pace. We just build the option.

## Your menu — do this, then this, then this

**Step 1 — See it.**
A live dashboard (Overview, Closers, Setters) that matches your existing numbers exactly. Nothing else changes. Your sheet and all your automations keep running untouched.
→ **Bill: no change.** This step is just about earning your trust.

**Step 2 — Own your data.**
We quietly start capturing your sales events into your *own* database — **in addition to** the sheet, never instead of it yet. Your automations keep writing to the sheet exactly as before; we simply also keep a copy that's yours. If anything ever looks wrong, the sheet is still the backup.
→ **Bill: roughly the same** (a tiny, temporary bump). This is the setup that unlocks the savings.

**Step 3 — Cut the waste.**
Now that the dashboard does the reporting and your database holds the data, we switch off the automations that were only **copying numbers around and sending internal pings**. The genuinely useful ones — the emails and texts that reach your customers, and the real connections between your tools — we keep, untouched.
→ **Bill: the spikes go away.**

Each step is optional and reversible. You can stop after Step 1 and still have a dashboard you love. Steps 2 and 3 are where the money comes back.

## What you don't lose

This is a "cut the waste, not the work" plan. Specifically:

- **Every customer email and text keeps sending.** The pre-call reminders, the follow-ups — all untouched.
- **Every real connection between your tools keeps working.** Where two tools genuinely need to talk to each other, that keeps running.
- **We never turn anything off until the new system has proven it does the same job** — and that's checked *automatically every night* against your own HUB totals, so nothing can quietly drift.
- **The sheet stays until you say otherwise.** It's your comfort backup for as long as you want it.
